A few important distinctions matter:

- General prenatal massage is usually considered safe in a healthy pregnancy when done by someone trained in pregnancy massage.
- There’s limited evidence that stimulating certain pressure points (sometimes discussed in acupressure) can trigger contractions, but studies are mixed and effects are generally not strong or predictable.
- Deep abdominal massage, intense pressure, or aggressive techniques should be avoided during pregnancy.
- If someone is high-risk, has bleeding, preterm contractions, placenta issues, high blood pressure, or has been told to avoid certain activities, they should check with their clinician before getting a massage.
